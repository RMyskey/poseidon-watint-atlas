Data-center water siting MODELED

A screening score for Texas that combines brackish groundwater, produced-water presence, water stress and data-center demand, inspired by the idea behind the UT Austin Sustainable Water Use Index (SWUI). It is not the paper's method (the full text was not accessed) and it is not a permitting, engineering or cost assessment. Change the weights to see how the ranking moves.

    About Poseidon NARINT / WATINT

    Poseidon NARINT is a concept prototype for water intelligence (WATINT): who has water, who is polluting it, where produced and waste water goes, and what new demand such as data centers competes for. It mirrors the structure of the Millennium KI Strategic Minerals Atlas: static site, curated sources, scheduled feeds, review queue.

    Every figure carries its source and as-of date. Nothing here was invented or imputed; gaps are listed in Method. This is an analytic prototype built only from open, unclassified public data.

    Poseidon NARINT concept prototype: open, unclassified public data only. Not an official product of the Department of War, USACE, or the Army Geospatial Center.
